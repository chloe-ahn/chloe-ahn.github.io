About

I am a PhD Candidate in Economics at the University of Bristol. My research focuses on household finance, housing, taxation, and inequality, with particular interest in household balance sheets, housing costs, and intergenerational differences in economic outcomes.

Before starting my PhD, I worked at the Bank of Korea, where I conducted economic research and statistical analysis across macroeconomics, international economics, national accounts, housing, productivity, and the digital economy.

Research

Job Market Paper

Priced Out: Housing Costs, Generational Wealth Gaps, and Youth Welfare

My job market paper studies the implications of rising housing costs for young households, wealth accumulation, and intergenerational inequality.
